Privacy Policy

Hotel Collection Chrome extension
Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Hotel Collection browser extension (“the Extension”) handles information. By using the Extension, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

Hotel Collection is a local-only tool. It helps you save hotels from Google Hotels / Google Maps into personal collections on your device, view them on a map, and export or share them as a JSON file that you control. The Extension does not require an account and does not operate a backend that receives your collection data.

2. Data we handle

When you choose to add a hotel, the Extension may read and store:

  • Website content from the Google Hotels/Maps page you are viewing (for example hotel name, rating, price text, image URL, and page URL).
  • Place location coordinates for that hotel (latitude / longitude), when available, so the hotel can be shown on your collection map. This is the hotel’s place location, not continuous tracking of your device GPS or IP address.
  • Optional notes, love/dislike votes, collection names, and list order that you enter in the Extension.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or other personal identity details
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Payment or financial information
  • Health information
  • Personal messages (email, chat, SMS)
  • A browse history of sites you visit over time
  • Keystrokes, mouse tracking, or network monitoring for analytics

3. How data is stored and used

  • Collection data is stored in chrome.storage.local on your device.
  • It is used only to provide Extension features: list, map, reorder, notes, export, import, and share.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it for credit scoring or unrelated purposes.

4. Sharing and transmission

  • The Extension does not upload your collection to our servers (there is no such server for this product).
  • Export / Share: If you use Export or Share, a JSON file is created on your device. You choose where to send it (for example WhatsApp, email, or Drive). That transfer is under your control.
  • Map tiles: The side-panel map may request map images from OpenStreetMap tile servers so pins can be displayed. Those requests are for map imagery, not to send your hotel list.
  • The Extension does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. Permissions (why they are needed)

  • storage — save collections on your device
  • contextMenus — “Add to collection” in the right-click menu
  • activeTab / scripting / tabs / host access — work on Google Hotels/Maps pages when you add a hotel, inject the Add button, and open saved hotels
  • sidePanel — show the collection UI beside the page

Host access is limited to Google Travel/Hotels, Google Maps, and OpenStreetMap tile hosts used for the map.

6. Children’s privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Extension changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when that happens. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
